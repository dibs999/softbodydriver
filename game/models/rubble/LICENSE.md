# Rubble vehicle models (3D models)

- Models: `sedan.glb`, `wagon.glb`, `police_sedan.glb`, `suv_4x4.glb`, `mach1_1969.glb`
- Author: **the Rubble authors** – generated by their own `engine/tools/vehicle-gen` scripts
  (no third-party meshes or textures).
- Source: https://github.com/Glavin001/rubble/tree/main/engine/scenes/vehicles
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC-BY-4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode)

Used unmodified as the visual flexbodies of `cars/meridian_sedan`, `meridian_wagon`,
`meridian_interceptor`, `trailhead_4x4` and `fastback_69`. The game deforms the meshes at runtime
from the soft-body simulation and may replace the `paint` and `glass` materials with its own
shaders. The designs carry no badges, logos or lettering; the vehicle names in this game are fictional.
